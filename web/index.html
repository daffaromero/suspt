A practice account for sharia-compliant US stocks. You get pretend money ($300 to start, $50 a month by default), buy and sell real stocks at real prices, and see how your choices would have done. No real money moves and nothing connects to a broker.

  • Sharia screen. Every company is checked with S&P's Shariah rules: limits on debt, cash and income from interest or haram business. Only compliant stocks can be bought, and if one you hold stops being compliant it is sold for you.
  • Every trade asks why. The journal later shows which reasons and themes made money, so you learn from your own decisions.
  • Benchmark. Your result is compared with simply buying SPUS, a sharia fund, with the same deposits.
  • Data refreshes by itself after each US market close while the app is open. The top-right button shows its progress.
